Your Winnowed Number: what you spend a year on subscriptions you chose on purpose.

Most people underestimate what they're paying for subscriptions by a lot. The streaming service you forgot. The tool you used twice. The membership from two years ago.

The usual answer is a spreadsheet, a dashboard of charts, or a cancellation concierge. None of them really work, because the problem isn't a tracking problem, it's a noticing problem. You need a moment, a few times a year, to sit quietly with the list and decide what still belongs.

Winnowed is built around that moment. Add your subscriptions by scanning a statement or typing them in. Then, at a cadence you choose, the app walks you through each one, card by card. Keep. Cut. Pause. That's it.

The ritual, from start to finish

  1. Scan a statement

    Point your camera at a bank statement. On devices with Apple Intelligence, Winnowed reads it on device and understands what it is looking at. Either way it finds the subscriptions and asks which ones to add.

  2. See the Stack

    Every subscription, grouped by what it costs over five years, from burgundy at the top down to sage.

  3. Review, card by card

    How often do you use it? Swipe right to keep, left to cut, and note why. New in 2.1, each review can carry that note.

  4. Get the Winnowed Number

    What you spend a year on subscriptions you chose on purpose. Everything you cut sits underneath it, counted.

  5. Keep the record

    Every review is saved, and so are price changes. History is a quiet ledger of what you decided and what it saved you.

The whole Stack, side by side

On iPad, and unfolded on iPhone Duo, Winnowed shows your Stack beside the subscription you're looking at. With iCloud on, every device keeps the same Stack. iPad is new in 2.2.

Also

Just ask

Ask Siri what you're spending this year, or what any one subscription costs you. Add a subscription or mark one reviewed without opening the app. Your Stack turns up in Spotlight, and every action works as a Shortcut.

A quiet reminder

The widget keeps the annual total on your Home Screen, and tells you when it is time to winnow again.

Yours, and nobody else's

Your Stack syncs through your own iCloud account. No accounts to make, no servers of ours, no tracking. Winnowed cannot see your data, and neither can anyone else.
